Hello, Nice to Meet You!
======
I'm Di'Carlo Jackson Jr., a passionate and driven student deeply fascinated by wildlife ecology. With an insatiable curiosity about our planet's diverse ecosystems and the complex interactions within them, I'm dedicated to enhancing our understanding of the natural world.

Welcome to my website! Here, you'll get a glimpse into my life and the incredible experiences I've had. You'll find a comprehensive collection of information about me, covering almost everything you need to know.

As a member of Penn State University's esteemed Ecology Master's program, I'm excited to be part of a community that values knowledge-sharing and global impact. I'm eager to expand my expertise and contribute to meaningful research that benefits both our environment and society. Join me on this journey to uncover the wonders of our planet and work towards a sustainable future!

About me
------
I am a Ph.D. student at The Pennsylvania State University interested in understanding the ecological and evolutionary biology, as well as life history strategies of animals. I am passionate about studying reproduction, phenology, the impact of changing climates and finding management strategies to conserve organisms across taxa.

I earned my M.S. in ecology at Penn State University and my B.Sc. in biology at Park University. There I participated in an independent research study on riverine turtle species diversity Parkville, MO. While at park I was also a student-athlete playing for the men's basketball team & apart of Park's Student Activates Board. While in undergrad I also participated in a NSF Undergraduate Research Experience program at the University of Toledo, where I studied the spatial ecology of Northern Map Turtles in Lake Erie using satellite telemetry.

For my M.S., I worked on a NSF-funded research project examining the life-history consequences of insular dwarfism in reptiles on California’s Channel Islands. My goal is to examine differences and similarities in reproductive characteristic between island and mainland populations to understand the impact of dwarfism on these life-history trade-offs.

In my free time I enjoy  exploring the outdoors, whether fishing, hiking, or herping, as well as cooking, working out, and playing basketball.
